# Privacy notice and acceptable-use drafts

**Draft — not approved for publication as the company’s legal policy.** Replace all unresolved company facts before use. The application's policy screen carries the same review warning.

## Privacy notice draft

Legal Doc Manager by 72BPM is used for Charge Point's authorized agreement administration: intake, preparation, negotiation, approvals, physical execution and security. Necessary information may include business contact names/email, organization and project details, commercial terms, documents, client-confirmation evidence and authenticated access/decision history. Do not provide Aadhaar/government identifiers, financial account information, health data or unrelated personal details unless an approved purpose and handling process separately authorizes them.

The company must identify its correct legal entity and privacy owner; itemize actual purposes/data; determine and document the applicable lawful processing grounds; and state appropriate recipients/processors, retention and transfer conditions. Consent is not presumed to cover all processing or to be the only lawful basis. Where consent is the approved basis, define notice, evidence, withdrawal and consequences clearly.

Access is limited by assigned role, organization and department. Google Workspace is the selected sign-in provider. AWS Mumbai is the selected deployment target; current local development and the separate design preview are not a verified production AWS deployment. Complete processor terms and actual cross-border access/identity-processing disclosures before launch.

Authorized internal users can submit access, correction, erasure or grievance requests through Privacy requests. The company must provide an approved public contact/channel for other individuals, verify identity/authority proportionately, define response and escalation procedures, and communicate reasoned outcomes. Contract retention, legal claims and holds may affect erasure; requests do not automatically delete documents. Specify any applicable future DPDP rights channels and commencement status with counsel.

**Outstanding company facts:** legal entity/address; privacy/grievance contact; applicable purposes/bases; exact data recipients/processors; retention schedules; transfer/access locations; rights and complaint procedures; effective date and approved notice version. No invented contact is supplied.

## Acceptable-use draft

Use your individual authorized account for approved business work only. Do not share credentials, expand access without authority, bypass permissions, impersonate client confirmation or signatory approval, upload malicious/unauthorized files, or export documents outside approved channels. Limit personal information to what the request needs. Downloaded files remain the user's responsibility and cannot be revoked from their device.

Report suspected exposure or misuse immediately through the company's designated incident channel once approved. Do not conduct unauthorized investigation, destroy evidence or send regulatory notices independently. Company policy must specify authorized reviewers, incident escalation, account offboarding, document retention, permitted devices and disciplinary/contractual consequences. This draft does not assert new binding terms or request acceptance.
